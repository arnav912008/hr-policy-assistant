# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, give and accept feedback graciously, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately (see the repository profile). Reports are handled confidentially.
